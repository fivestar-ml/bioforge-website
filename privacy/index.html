Privacy Policy

BioForge is designed to be playable offline without accounts, ads, tracking, analytics, or cloud services.

Information We Collect

BioForge does not collect personal information.

The app stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including:

  • Stage progress
  • Coins and permanent upgrades
  • Codex discovery, mastery, and applied concept progress
  • Settings such as sound, haptics, and reduced motion

This progress is stored using Apple's local app storage and is not transmitted by BioForge.

Tracking And Analytics

BioForge does not use tracking SDKs, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Accounts And Online Services

BioForge does not require an account and does not include multiplayer, chat, cloud saves, leaderboards, or social features in the v1 launch scope.

Children

BioForge is designed to be education-friendly, but it is not submitted as a dedicated Kids Category app unless that decision is made separately in App Store Connect.

Data Sharing

BioForge does not sell, rent, or share personal data because it does not collect personal data.

Deleting Progress

Players can reset local progress from the in-app Settings screen. Deleting the app also removes local app data according to normal iOS behavior.

Changes To This Policy

If BioForge adds online services, analytics, accounts, or other data collection in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy details must be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact support@bioforge.app.
