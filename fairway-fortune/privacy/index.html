Privacy Policy

Fairway Fortune stores game progress and uses Apple and advertising services for optional cloud save, purchases, and rewarded ads.

Information We Use

Fairway Fortune stores gameplay progress on your device, including course upgrades, managers, currencies, achievements, offline earnings, legacy progress, and settings.

If you connect Apple Game Center, save progress can sync through Game Center. Game Center is provided by Apple and is controlled by your Apple account settings.

Purchases

Golden Ball purchases use Apple StoreKit. Purchase status and transaction identifiers are used to deliver purchased in-game currency, restore purchase state when needed, and prevent duplicate delivery.

Rewarded Ads

Fairway Fortune includes optional rewarded ads. Ad delivery may use device identifiers and ad network signals handled by the ad provider to load ads, deliver rewards, prevent fraud, and measure ad functionality.

Rewarded ads are optional and are not required to play.

Tracking And Sharing

Fairway Fortune does not sell personal information. The app does not require a ZboxGames account and does not include chat, user-generated content, or multiplayer features.

Data is used for app functionality, purchases, cloud save, and optional rewarded ads.

Deleting Progress

You can reset local progress from the in-app Settings screen. Deleting the app also removes local app data according to normal iOS behavior. Game Center data is managed through Apple services and Apple account settings.

Changes To This Policy

If Fairway Fortune adds accounts, analytics, new advertising behavior, or other data collection in the future, this policy and App Store privacy details must be updated before release.
