Help and FAQ

Use this page for support, bug reports, troubleshooting, and common questions about Fairway Fortune.

Contact

Email zboxgamessupport@gmail.com for support.

When reporting a bug, include your device model, iOS version, Fairway Fortune version and build number, what screen you were on, and what happened before the issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fairway Fortune playable offline?

Yes. The game stores progress locally and calculates offline earnings when you return.

Does Fairway Fortune require an account?

No. A ZboxGames account is not required. Game Center can be used for Apple-provided save sync when connected.

How are purchases handled?

Golden Ball packs are handled through Apple StoreKit. If a purchase does not appear immediately, reopen the shop after checking your network connection.

Are rewarded ads required?

No. Rewarded ads are optional and are used only when you choose to watch one for a bonus, such as doubling offline earnings when available.

How do offline rewards work?

Fairway Fortune calculates eligible idle earnings while the app is closed, up to the current offline cap shown in game.

Can I reset progress?

Yes. Open Settings in the app and use Reset Save.

Why can some upgrade branches scroll?

Large upgrade maps are designed to be scrollable so branches can stay readable without overlapping.
